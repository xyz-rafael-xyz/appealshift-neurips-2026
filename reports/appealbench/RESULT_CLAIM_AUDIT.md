# AppealShift result claim audit

## Admission rule

Every quantitative statement in the form abstract and technical report must appear in recorded analysis or be a direct integer conversion of a reported rate. The plausible-source run has its own complete-grid validator and independent scorer. The matched valid controls and their protocol are included in the artifact. The public artifact history does not establish their order. The earlier four-state checker reads raw JSONL without importing the main analysis. It passed 1,080 of 1,080 checks, including all five leave-one-model-out rows.

## Claims excluded

The results do not show that denial rationales are generally beneficial or harmful. They do not establish human anchoring or institutional fairness. They say nothing about legal remedy. No real applicant or humanitarian organization was studied. The audit packet has no human labels. No causal statement extends beyond the frozen prompts and model revisions.

The required `INELIGIBLE` label is an operational consequence of fictional clause C2. It is not presented as a generally humane or institutionally correct response. Practitioner review of that choice remains requested but has not occurred.
